--- test/test_nn.py:13516 (3985158698): PASTE BEGIN ---

Agreed, rewritten to check both precisely. With `dtype=torch.float32` the total of the whole tensor and of the split `[g[:2], g[2:]]` both equal the exactly rounded norm (288 for p=1, `sqrt(66050)` for p=2). Without `dtype` (current behavior) the result is bfloat16 and, for p=2, depends on the split: 258 for the whole tensor vs 256 for the split, because the first part's norm 257 rounds to 256 before the parts are combined. That split dependence is what the new argument removes.
