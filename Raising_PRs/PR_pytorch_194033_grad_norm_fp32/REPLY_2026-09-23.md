On point 3: the bot is right that the exact bfloat16 values are CPU and CUDA results, so the test now runs only there (`@onlyOn(["cpu", "cuda"])`) and keeps 258 and 256, which both kernels give by accumulating bfloat16 in float32. On "dtype is constrained, and the docstring doesn't say so" under 4: agreed, and the docstring now says it. For real inputs `dtype` must be a floating point dtype the inputs promote to (`torch.promote_types(input.dtype, dtype) == dtype`), the check `linalg.vector_norm` and the foreach CUDA kernel make. For example, float32 gradients with `dtype=torch.bfloat16` raise instead of accumulating in lower precision.
