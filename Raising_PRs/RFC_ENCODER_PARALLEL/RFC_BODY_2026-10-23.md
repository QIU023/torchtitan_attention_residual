## Summary

In torchtitan's vision-language models the vision encoder repeats its work on every CP rank, sits on the first pipeline stage, and carries whatever media load each data-parallel rank's samples bring. This RFC proposes turning dynamic CP (#4380) and the decoupled encoder process, DEP (#4381), today Kimi K3 only, into model-generic modules behind one encoder contract, with Kimi K3 and Kimi K2.5 as the first users and the other vision-language models able to follow as soon as it lands.

## The encoder under each parallelism dimension

| Dimension | Main today | Known techniques | This RFC |
|---|---|---|---|
| DP / FSDP | Each DP rank encodes its own samples; packing fills micro-batches by sequence length after DP sharding (`TODO(data-global-pack-plan)` in `mm_datasets.py`). | Images or tiles balanced across DP ranks (DeepSeek-VL2, Seed1.5-VL); the whole batch's vision forward balanced over all GPUs (Kimi K2.5). | Covered, not implemented |
| TP | The common vision sharding configs shard the encoder's linears; every TP rank runs every patch. | The encoder on its own data parallelism (Seed1.5-VL); a smaller TP degree for the encoder (Megatron-Core). | Covered, not implemented |
| CP | Every CP rank encodes the full vision input (`multimodal_input_sharding`). | Dynamic CP: a large image split by patches over a sub-CP group with a KV gather (Kimi K3). | #4380 |
| PP | The encoder sits on the first stage (`pipeline_first_stage_module_fqns`). | DEP: encoder copies on every rank with a balanced forward, recompute and backward (Kimi K2.5), placed in pipeline bubbles (Kimi K3, Optimus); the encoder's layers divided across stages (DeepSeek-VL2). | #4381 |
| EP | The encoders are dense and run on the data dimensions EP is carved from. | None needed. | No change |

## Evidence from #4380 and #4381

The numbers below come from the two PR descriptions; both PRs will be re-measured on current main when review starts.

Dynamic CP (#4380): the Kimi K3 encoder alone (27 layers, dim 1024), forward and backward over one micro-batch's images on H100. Main encodes every image on every CP rank, so it measures the same at any CP degree. Cells are peak memory per GPU / time:

| Images (patches) | main | CP=2 | CP=4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| one 4032 px image (82944) | 55.2 GiB / 10.9 s | 28.5 GiB / 5.6 s | 15.3 GiB / 2.9 s |
| four 2016 px images (82944) | 55.2 GiB / 3.1 s | 28.6 GiB / 1.6 s | 15.3 GiB / 0.80 s |
| 16 frames of 448 px (16384) | 11.6 GiB / 0.22 s | 6.3 GiB / 0.14 s | 3.6 GiB / 0.13 s |

DEP (#4381): 4 H100s, pp4 x vpp4, Interleaved1F1B with 16 micro-batches, the Kimi K3 debug model widened to dim 6144. Cells are the share of the encoder's forward and backward kernel time that runs inside pipeline bubbles in one traced step:

| seq | image side | encoder forward / text stage forward | K2.5 form | K3 form (bubbles) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2048 | 224 px | 0.046 | 1% | 71% |
| 2048 | 1008 px | 0.136 | 6% | 71% |
| 1536 | 1008 px | 0.169 | 0% | 69% |

- With one equal image per micro-batch, the plan can place at most `1 - p/M` of the encoder work in bubbles (p pipeline ranks, M micro-batches): 75% for this pipeline.
- Kimi K2.5 reports 90% of text-only training efficiency for multimodal training with DEP.
- With dynamic CP off, #4380 trains bitwise to main for 100 steps.

## Proposal

- An encoder contract in `models/common/vision_encoder.py`:
  - `forward(pixel_values, *, grid_thw)` over packed items, returning the bank rows in item order (every encoder in the tree already has this signature);
  - the output rows per item and the merge kernel;
  - for dynamic CP, the position embeddings and the merge of a band of rows.
- Two opt-in wrappers in common, off by default:
  - Dynamic CP swaps the encoder's class for a CP subclass the way `fully_shard` does, so FQNs and checkpoints do not change, and adds the KV gather to the common `VisionAttention`.
  - DEP runs in `MultimodalModel.pipeline()`: it builds the stages as today, swaps their class for a DEP subclass, wraps the schedule's `step`, and feeds precomputed vision embeddings to the model.
  - The planners, gather, runtime, stage and schedule move out of the Kimi K3 folder unchanged.
- Users:
  - First, Kimi K3 and Kimi K2.5, which share the MoonViT encoder, so #4380's band positions and merge serve both.
  - Then Qwen3.5 (band positions and merge for its `PatchMerger`), and Muse Spark and Muse Glimmer (for DEP, the encoder, adapter and projection replicated together).
- Evaluation: wall-clock step time and tokens per second per GPU (not MFU, see #4009), and peak memory and stage busy time on every rank, on images of 2K to 4K px, video of 16 to 64 frames, a packed mix with text-only samples, and a text-heavy control. With a feature off, runs stay bitwise to main.

Feedback on the contract and on the evaluation workloads is welcome here; the details can follow on the PRs.

## Approaches in other model reports

- DeepSeek-VL2: fine-grained layer division of the vision encoder within pipeline parallelism, image tile load balancing across DP ranks in forward and backward, and separate pipeline strategies for text-only and image batches.
- Seed1.5-VL: the encoder and adapter on ZeRO data parallelism and the language model on 4D parallelism; images assigned greedily to the least loaded GPU within groups (for example, of 192 GPUs).
- Qwen2.5-VL: windowed attention in the encoder against the load imbalance of native resolution inputs, and packing that balances the language model's load. Neither it nor Qwen3-VL describes encoder-specific parallelism.
- GLM-4.5V and GLM-4.1V-Thinking: 3D convolutions downsample video by two in time inside the encoder; DP balancing and re-packing are described for the RL stage.
- MiniMax-VL-01: the MiniMax-01 report describes no encoder-specific training infrastructure.
