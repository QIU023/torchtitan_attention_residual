The channel is `clip_grad_norm_`: under PP each rank's norm is squared, the squares are all-reduced and the root taken, a different summation order from one device's single `vector_norm`, and the clip factor (the norm is above `max_norm` on the first 17 steps) carries that one fp32 unit into the parameters. With the per-parameter norms gathered over the pp ranks and reduced in the single device order (a probe switch, not in the PR) the same cell is bitwise with the gradient accumulation on all 100 steps, loss, grad norm and the norm's bits. ## Thread 6, `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/model.py:242` (discussion_r4056308907)

--- PASTE BEGIN ---

The summary in the review above has it: at 2 blocks x 4 layers and no PP, the end of the forward holds three stack columns, one [T, D] partial sum per layer, and, unless AC recomputes them, the per layer fp32 residual internals, which are the cost that matters.

Measured on that shape with `saved_tensors_hooks`, still allocated after the forward above the parameters: 383 MiB with no AC, 63 MiB with `SelectiveAC` (the flavor's default) and 15.5 MiB with full AC, of which the stack columns are 1.5 MiB.

#4780 takes that term out instead of recomputing it, so the numbers stop depending on AC: the aggregation becomes an autograd Function saving references plus three fp32 vectors of [N + 1, T] per call, and at the released hidden size one call retains 0.2 MiB where this code retains 1008.2, with a forward peak of 280 MiB against 1764. It is filed against main, since the same function is the head's aggregation, so this diff does not move.
