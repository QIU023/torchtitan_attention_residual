lora: adapters keep the base weight's placement outside TP

_adapter_sharding compared the base weight's placement with dense_param_placement(tp=S(0)) and asserted that every
other base was dense_param_placement(tp=S(1)). Two kinds of base failed that assert at build, so LoRATransform with its
default targets could not build deepseek_v3, deepseek_v4, gpt_oss, qwen3_5, kimi_k2_5 or kimi_k3: bases that TP does
not shard (R when tokens are TP-sharded, I otherwise), such as the MoE router gate and the MLA down projections, and the
vision encoder's column and row parallel linears, which are I rather than R on CP.

The adapters now follow the TP entry of the base placement: S(0) and S(1) keep the column and row rules, R and I give
both adapters the base placement, and any other TP placement raises. The replicated adapter copies the base placement
with TP set to R, so its DP and CP entries are the base's; for a dense base that is dense_param_placement(tp=R) as before.
