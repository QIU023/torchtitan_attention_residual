lora: export the adapters alone or folded into the base weights

trainable_state_dict returns the parameters left trainable, keyed as in state_dict(): under LoRA, the adapters, which
is what an adapter checkpoint or a trainer-to-rollout sync ships. merge_lora_state_dict returns state_dict() with each
base weight replaced by W + (alpha / rank) * B @ A and the adapter keys removed, the form a state-dict adapter can
convert like the unadapted model's.

The merge runs in fp32 on each rank's local shards and casts back to the base dtype. B is sharded like the base's
output rows, A is gathered only where FSDP cuts its rank dimension, and the result keeps the base's DTensor placements,
so no base weight is gathered. DTensor ops on the sharded tensors fail on a stacked w13 under FSDP and TP, whose
_StridedShard placement cannot be unflattened.
