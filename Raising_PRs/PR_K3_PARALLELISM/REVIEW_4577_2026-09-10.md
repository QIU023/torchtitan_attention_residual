# Review comments for #4577 (Shuhua's quantile balancing), 2026-09-10

Line numbers are in the PR head `bf3058eae`; each block is one review comment, paste as is. The context: `phase13_k3like_48b_posttrain/QB_4577_VS_4412_2026-09-10.md`.

--- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/quantile_balance.py` L182 (`estimate_expert_bias`, `fraction_E = (target_count_E - counts_before_E.float()) / counts_in_bin_E.float()`) ---

`counts_in_bin_E` is zero whenever an expert's histogram is empty at the pre-hook: `target_count_E` is then 0, `target_rank_E` 0, `target_bin_E` 0 and `counts_in_bin_E = histogram[e, 0] = 0`, so `fraction_E` is NaN and the `copy_` on L250 writes NaN into `expert_bias_E`, which the next step routes with. The histogram is empty for any MoE layer whose router ran no training forward since the last solve; the current train step always forwards before it steps, so this needs a resume or a schedule that steps a stage without a forward to show, but the guard is one line: `fraction_E = torch.where(counts_in_bin_E > 0, ..., 0.0)` (or skip the solve when `counts_E.sum() == 0`), and #4412's `_armed` flag is the same idea at the hook level. For the crossing bin itself the division is safe: `target_bin_E` is the first bin whose cumulative count reaches the rank, so it holds at least one count unless the rank is 0.

--- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/quantile_balance.py` L221 (`_all_reduce_histograms`, the per-layer `all_reduce` list) ---

One collective per MoE layer per optimizer step: at K3's size that is 92 int32 all-reduces of `(896, 1000)` per step, twice with EP x TP (dense-tp mesh, then loss mesh). The histograms have one shape, so `torch.stack` over the layers, one `all_reduce` of `(L, E, B)`, and a `copy_` back (or keep the stacked buffer as the store and give each router a view) is the same bytes in one call; #4412 does that on the loss mesh. Async issue-then-wait hides the latency but not the launch count.

--- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/quantile_balance.py` L90 (`self.quantile_balancer.observe(scores_TE, cutoff_T1, expert_bias_E)` in `forward`) ---

Under selective activation checkpointing the router forward is replayed in backward: `_select_experts_and_cutoff` is protected by `remat.region(..., recompute=False)`, but `observe` sits outside it and runs again on the replay, so a recomputed micro-batch is counted twice. The quantile is invariant to a uniform factor (rank and fraction both scale), so the bias is unchanged, but the int32 histogram fills twice as fast and the docstring's "each training micro-batch is accumulated once" is not what happens with SAC on. Worth a sentence in the docstring, or an `if torch.is_grad_enabled() and not <in recompute>` guard if torch_remat exposes one.

--- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/quantile_balance.py` L94 (`class QuantileBalancer(Module)`), or a top-level comment ---

+1 to Jeremalloch's point: the histogram, the solve and the pre-hook do not read anything Kimi-specific (sigmoid scores, top-k, a bias buffer), and Marin and Megatron keep the same algorithm architecture-agnostic. `QuantileBalancer` and `register_moe_quantile_balancing_hook` fit in `torchtitan/models/common/moe.py` and `torchtitan/components/optimizer` next to the sign rule, with the MoE's balancing method chosen by config (`load_balance_coeff` vs a quantile config) instead of the `del self.expert_bias_E` re-registration in `KimiLatentMoE.__init__` (moe.py L125); `kimi_k3` then only picks the router config and the `post_optimizer_build_fn`.
