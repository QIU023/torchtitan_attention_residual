kimi_k3: each block is stored once per pipeline rank and freed at its last reader

Each hop sends one tensor per block, and the model takes and returns its
blocks as a list, so the rank store keeps references to the received and
committed tensors instead of copies, and no stage stacks its blocks into a
fresh leaf. The store drops each block at the backward of the stage that
brought it onto the rank, which is its last reader there.

Under the action-list runtime a pending send keeps its tensor alive, so the
stage waits a forward send at its own backward of that micro-batch and an
input-gradient send at the first forward that proves the peer has used it,
instead of at the end of the step. Receive buffers stay with torch, which
allocates each one right before its receive.
