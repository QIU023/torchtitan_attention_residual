--- pipeline_parallel/__init__.py:67 ---
--- PASTE BEGIN ---
Guarded now, on the map rather than on the schedule class. With `attn_res_cache` on, `pipeline_kimi_k3` walks the schedule's `stage_index_to_group_rank` and requires `rank == stage % pp` for every stage, the loop-style assignment the rank store assumes; the first stage that breaks it raises a `ValueError` naming the schedule and that stage, and points at a looped schedule or `attn_res_cache` off. That admits `1F1B`, `GPipe`, `Interleaved1F1B`, `InterleavedZeroBubble`, `LoopedBFS` and a CSV schedule whose layout happens to be loop-style, and refuses `ZBVZeroBubble`, `DualPipeV` and any other CSV layout, which no class name would catch. Without the cache every hop carries the whole stack and any assignment works, so the check is scoped to the cache. Two CPU tests cover it, a loop map passing and a v-shaped one refused.
--- PASTE END ---
