## Reply (final; the body's Results section carries the same tables)

Yes: every cell trains on 8192 tokens per step in 256-token micro-batches, from one seed checkpoint.

The gap is between rows, not backends. The loader shards documents by dp rank (`components/data/sources.py`, `split_dataset_by_node`), so the dp1 and dp2 rows read different samples: their step-1 losses already differ (12.52977 vs 12.53137) with identical weights, and they train on different data from step 2 on. The comparison this PR needs is the same cell under both backends, and that is bitwise through all ten steps:

| cell | world | partial_dtensor (step 1 / 3 / 10) | spmd_types (step 1 / 3 / 10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| dp1 | 1 | 12.52977 / 7.27107 / 2.98077 | 12.52977 / 7.27107 / 2.98077 |
| dp2 | 2 | 12.53137 / 7.31248 / 3.15823 | 12.53137 / 7.31248 / 3.15823 |
| dp2 x ep2 | 2 | 12.53146 / 7.20212 / 3.10296 | 12.53146 / 7.20212 / 3.10296 |

Step-1 gradients of the same cell under the two backends, every parameter on rank 0: 750/750 bitwise, zero sign flips over 1.12e9 elements, on dp2 and on dp2 x ep2.

What is left is this flavor's own spread, not a backend's: bf16 end to end at lr 8e-4 with a 2-step warm-up, so Adam's first update is lr times sign(g), and any two runs that round differently separate by a few percent by step 10. Two measurements of that floor: the same dp1 cell on another compile cache moves 1.3% at step 3 and 2.4% at step 10 with bitwise step-1 gradients; EP on with the same samples flips the sign of 1.4% of the gradient elements at step 1. I have rebuilt the body's table as same-data pairs on one compile cache with that floor, and relabelled the last three rows as 4446's CI cell (`_use_spmd_types(typechecking=True)`, which turns AC off), the cell this PR unbreaks.
