## 3. `context_parallel.py:70` -- MLA Ulysses (short form, posted after threads 1 and 4 were answered on 2026-09-06)

No, the boundary is clean and rope is applied before it; the kernel takes q, k, v as they arrive. The only difference is what travels: the rope slice is the same on every head, so the generic all-to-all carries it once per head, while this kernel sends `q | k_nope | v` in one all-to-all and the rope slice once (a small all-gather): fewer bytes (12% at K3's shapes) and one collective instead of three, not extra comms.

vs all-gather KV: the all-to-all moves 1/cp of the tensor per rank, the all-gather the whole K/V; equal at cp = 2, better as cp grows, and it needs `n_heads % cp == 0`.

`fused_mla`: yes. The override is specific to DeepSeek-V3's `Attention` (it does not attach to `KimiMLAAttention`), so I tested it there: `deepseek_v3_debugmodel` at dp1, cp2 on the upstream Ulysses kernel and cp2 on this kernel, each with and without `--override.imports torchtitan.overrides.fused_mla.fused_mla`, are bitwise their stock twins through step 3 (packed vs generic: step 1 bitwise, step 3 within 3e-5); activation checkpointing off, which the override needs (it mutates tensors selective AC caches).

On where this lives: it already follows @fegin's first option (split inside the kernel, the standard q/k/v interface) and I would keep it that way; happy to move the two classes to `models/common/cp_attention.py` if you want deepseek_v3 to share them.
