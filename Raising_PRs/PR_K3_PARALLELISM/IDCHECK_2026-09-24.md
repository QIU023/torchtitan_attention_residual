# PR 4312: do the body tables need an H100 rerun after the round 5 rebase? (2026-09-24)

No. The body tables were measured on 4 x H100 at `ce67cece4` (main `7349a2282`). Against it, `8a3fe2954` (round 5 on
main `9e159aed7`; the PR head `e592286b4` differs from it only in a comment) gives the same loss and grad norm on every
step of ten of the body's cells, 100 steps each, on one compile cache per cell: both transports, pp2 and pp4, one to
four stages per rank, dp1 and dp2. Not rerun: the naive rows at the other shapes, dp2 x pp2, and the single-device
controls (stock accumulation, reversed micro-batches, cold fill); they take the code paths the ten cover.

Protocol (8 x RTX 5060 Ti, venv_bfx9, scratch `idcheck/`): the H100 kit's own probe (`probe_apply_h100.py`) applied to
a worktree of each head, with two additions on both: the kit's c4 flavor sets `default_adamw(lr=8e-4)` (it inherits
`kimi_k3_debugmodel()`, which was AdamW at `ce67cece4` and is per-head DistMuon since #4596), and the 16-stage flavor
spells out the removed pp4 x vp4 recipe's split (checked equal to the old recipe's). The kit's settings: seed 42,
deterministic, seed checkpoint per batch shape (made once, loaded by both heads), GN_FP32, the references with
NOSYNC_GA run cold then warm. Old head first; the new head runs each cell on a copy of the old head's finished cache for
that cell, so equal graphs meet equal kernels. The KDA guard is lifted by the probe (SM120 here).

Why, from the diffs (7349a2282..9e159aed7 plus round 5): #4704 (TP projection backends) is the identity at tp = 1
(Column/RowParallelLinear reduce to the same `F.linear`, same FQNs, shapes, bias and init; MLA/KDA redistribute only
with a TP group); #4837 adds remat regions only to the EP > 1 dispatchers (`LocalTokenDispatcher` becomes a
parameterless Module); #4840 and #4476 are DistMuon and graph_trainer; #4596 changes only the recipe's optimizer; the
round 5 commits rename, move, raise on CP, comment, and take the same split through `get_module_fqns_per_model_part`.

What does change: rerunning the kit on the current head needs the AdamW line in the c4 flavor (otherwise it trains
DistMuon, and the head-alone 16-stage split fails DistMuon's "matched no parameters") and the 16-stage split spelled out
(the recipe it imported is gone). The body should say the tables use AdamW, the recipe's optimizer before #4596.
