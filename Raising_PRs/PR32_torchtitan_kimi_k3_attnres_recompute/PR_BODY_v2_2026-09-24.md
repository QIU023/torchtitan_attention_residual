## Summary

Two corrections to Kimi K3's attention residual, the depth-softmax aggregation that #4025 introduced, and a fused kernel for it behind the override mechanism.

Kimi K3 layers do not add their input to their output: each layer takes a weighted average of the earlier blocks' outputs and the running sum, with weights from a softmax over depth, `score = query · RMSNorm(source)`; the query is the learned `*_res_proj.weight`.

- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/__init__.py`: the three residual projections initialise to zero. Section 5 of the Kimi Linear report starts every pseudo-query at zero, so the depth softmax starts uniform and the residual starts as a plain average; they were drawn from `trunc_normal_` at std 0.02.
- `torchtitan/models/kimi_k3/model.py`: the aggregation becomes `AttentionResidual`, a parameter-free `Configurable` `Function` whose default is the eager FP32 form main has today. Each block holds one for its attention residual and one for its feed-forward residual, and the model one for the output residual, so the override mechanism has a node to swap. The norm and projection weights stay where they were; checkpoints, the state dict adapter and the sharding config are untouched.
- `torchtitan/overrides/fused_attnres.py`: `--override.imports torchtitan.overrides.fused_attnres.fused_attnres` swaps every aggregation for flash-linear-attention's fused Triton op, which runs one pass over the sources per token tile with an online softmax and saves only per-token statistics, rebuilding the FP32 upcasts in backward. `fla` is optional (`flash-linear-attention` at or above 0.6.0, which introduced `fla.ops.attnres`); without it, or off CUDA, the aggregation falls back to the eager form. For SPMD type checking the op is registered with its eager decomposition, so the checker traces plain ops and never runs the kernel.
- Tests: `test_kimi_k3_attention_residual.py` (zero initialisation, the aggregation as a config node) and `test_fused_attnres_override.py` (the override replaces every node, the CPU fallback, and on CUDA with fla the fused op against the eager form, forward and all four gradients).

## Why the eager form stays the default

The eager aggregation keeps two FP32 copies of the `[tokens, stack, hidden]` stack alive until backward, about 1 GiB per call at `dim` 7168 with a stack of 8 and 2048 tokens; with activation checkpointing they are built again in recompute. A hand-written recomputing `autograd.Function` was tried first and dropped: it ran op by op and was slower in forward. The fused kernel is the right shape for a memory-bound reduction over depth, and it is a third-party Triton kernel, so it lives behind an override rather than in the model file.

## Results

fla's op against the eager form on random inputs: forward and all four gradients agree to 1e-5 relative in bfloat16 and 1e-7 in float32; at zero initialisation both return the mean of the sources and the norm weight gets no gradient.
