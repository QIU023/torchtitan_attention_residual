# Finding 54 converged apply_fsdp and deadlocked every multimodal CP cell

The convergence (`3be16007b`) replaced our 182-line `apply_fsdp` with a call to
`distributed.fsdp.apply_fsdp_to_decoder`. It passed the CPU suite, passed a live smoke on
`fsdp2` / `ep2_fsdp2` / `fsdp2_pp2`, and produced BIT-IDENTICAL numbers on `fsdp2` and
`ep2_fsdp2`. Then the matrix gate found what none of that could.

## The failure

Three cells that passed before now hang and get killed by the NCCL watchdog:

    cp2                FAIL (0/10)
    fsdp2_tp2_cp2      FAIL (0/10)
    ep2_fsdp2_tp2_cp2  FAIL (0/10)
    cp4                FAIL (0/10)      (max-degree)

    Watchdog caught collective operation timeout: WorkNCCL(SeqNum=149,
    OpType=_ALLGATHER_BASE, NumelIn=3091200, NumelOut=6182400, Timeout(ms)=300000)
    #2 __call__   from torch/distributed/fsdp/_fully_shard/_fsdp_collectives.py:119
    #3 foreach_all_gather from torch/distributed/fsdp/_fully_shard/_fsdp_collectives.py:364

A deadlock in an FSDP all-gather, not a numerics change. **Every other cell in both arms is
byte-identical**, which is what makes the cause narrow rather than diffuse.

## Attribution, by controls rather than by reading

| arm | result |
|---|---|
| `cp2` on the commit BEFORE the convergence | passes, 12.03873 / 11.98521 |
| `cp2` on the convergence | hangs on `_ALLGATHER_BASE` |
| `cp2` TEXT flavor on the convergence | **passes**, 7.70116 / 7.71964 / 7.68079, identical to its baseline |

So: caused by the convergence, and multimodal only.

## Why multimodal only

The multimodal flavors hand `apply_fsdp` the WRAPPER (`vision_tower` +
`language_model`), which has `layers` but no `embed_tokens` / `norm` / `lm_head`. The
helper reads those three directly, so the convergence handed it `model.language_model`
instead and root-wrapped the wrapper afterwards.

That inserts an EXTRA FSDP unit -- `language_model` -- between the per-layer units and the
root. On a text flavor `decoder is model`, no extra unit appears, and text is unaffected.
Under CP the extra level is what desynchronises the all-gather sequence.

Worth stating plainly: the desk assessment in `REUSE_DIFF_TABLES_2026-08-10.md` said this
convergence needed only three read-only properties. That was right about the CONTRACT and
silent about the wrapper, which is a second, structural problem the property list could not
have exposed. Reading the helper's attribute reads told me what it needs; it did not tell me
what it does with what I hand it.

## The candidate fix

Alias on the WRAPPER instead of handing over the inner model: give
`KimiK3MultimodalModel` delegating `tok_embeddings` / `norm` / `lm_head` /
`enable_weight_tying` properties and call the helper on the wrapper. No extra unit, no
second root wrap. It still moves multimodal grouping -- embed / norm / lm_head become their
own units where the old code left them inside the root -- so mm numbers are expected to
move, which is the accepted cost of the convergence. Under test in a worktree.

If that also hangs, the convergence gets reverted and the aliases go with it; the
assessment and this document stay, because the useful part is knowing that the wrapper --
not the naming contract -- is what makes this hard.

## Reading note

The smoke tests that preceded the matrix (`fsdp2`, `ep2_fsdp2`, `fsdp2_pp2`) could not have
caught this: none of them enables CP. A three-shape smoke chosen for speed missed the one
axis the change breaks, and "bit-identical on two cells" read as reassurance. The gate is
the matrix.
