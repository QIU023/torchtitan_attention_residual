## Config-driven conversion (branch `migrate_stream_dtensor`)

Why: every other model carries a per-model `sharding.py` and has **zero** imperative
`ColwiseParallel`/`RowwiseParallel` entries (llama3 74 lines, qwen3 111, gpt_oss 127,
kimi_k2_7 145, deepseek_v3 192, qwen3_5 343). Ours is 1598 lines of `parallelize.py` with
ten. The idiom sets declarations on a **config tree** before `build()`, which needs a
config-driven model.

Done: 11 classes (`KimiMLP`, `KimiLatentMoEProjection`, `KimiMLAAttention`,
`KimiDeltaAttention`, `KimiMoE`, `KimiDecoderLayer`, `KimiAttnResDecoderLayer`,
`KimiK3Model`, plus every leaf construction site). Our `Linear`/`RMSNorm` adapters are
**deleted** -- all 172 Linear and 74 RMSNorm instances are core's classes now. 54/54 and
**loss identical to the pre-conversion baseline** on four cells.

Left: `KimiK3AttnResModel`, `KimiK3MTPLayer`, and `KimiK3Spec` becoming a flavor builder.
`KimiK3Spec` is hand-rolled and deliberately not a `BaseModel.Config`; core's
`set_decoder_sharding_config` needs `tok_embeddings`, `norm`, `lm_head` (which
`KimiK3Model.Config` now has) and the caller loops `config.layers` itself.

Three mechanics that will recur:

* the enclosing class must be torchtitan's `Module`, not `nn.Module` -- `Config.build`
  resolves its target through `_owner`, auto-wired by `Configurable.__init_subclass__`,
  and the failure reads as `Module.__init__() got an unexpected keyword argument 'config'`;
* converting a parent retires the adapters for its children by itself, because
  `Linear.Config` belongs to core's `Linear`;
* `model.config` must survive: `register_topology(model.config)` is read in parallelize
  and in the pipeline adapter.

## Faithful DEP (branch `dep_exp_impl`)

Three layers. **Decoupling**: `KimiK3ViTStage` on its own stage(s), `vit_dep_stages` --
our generalization, the report gives no count, so a value above 1 is never fidelity.
**Forward concurrency**, two alternatives, refused together: `KIMI_VIT_PREFETCH`
(side stream) and `KIMI_VIT_BUBBLE` (planned encodes on the main stream in idle
intervals). **Backward**: `cut_for_deferred_backward` detaches the tower's output,
splices the detached leaf, captures the gradient with a tensor hook, and the tower's
graph is replayed at an idle slot with an unconditional drain at step end.

Proven, at pp=8 / `layers_per_stage=1` (32 stages, vp=4) / Interleaved1F1B / 24
micro-batches / seq 256:

| claim | evidence |
| --- | --- |
| encodes run at planned points | 8/8 planned, 8 upfront, 8 synchronous |
| vision backwards run at planned slots | 24 ran, 0 drained |
| forward numerically unchanged | loss identical, multimodal and LoRA |
| backward numerically unchanged | grad_norm identical, 8 ranks x 6 steps, both flavors |
| no gradient lost | unit tests in `tests/test_dep_bubble.py` (13) |

**Hiding is NOT proven, and occupancy is not hiding.** The cell above was given
`COST_RATIO=0.493`, measured at seq **4096**; at seq 256 the same probe puts one ViT
forward at about **14** text-stage forwards. The encodes ran where planned and overran
the interval roughly 28-fold. With the honest ratio the planner places **0** at seq 256,
which is the correct answer and what the gate now passes.

Full reasoning, the three open items and the theory's prediction (saving peaks near
r = 0.5, zero by r = 10) are in `DEP_BUBBLE_STATUS_2026-08-16.md`. The blocker for all
three is one machine: seq 4096 for r near 0.5, interleaved vp >= 2, and micro-batches >=
stages where **the micro-batch count IS `local_batch_size`** -- so local batch >= 16 at
pp8/vp2, and seq 4096 x local 8 already OOMs in 15.5 GiB. About **60 GiB per GPU**.

## The review's 20 items

**A1 -- done, `8a6a86f2a`.** Real, and its stated impact was wrong in a way worth
knowing. `apply_ep_kimi_k3` read `layer.ffn` after the attribute became `layer.moe`, so
it logged "EP plan wrapped 0 MoE layer experts" through every green EP cell. Fixing the
name immediately produced "MoE has already been parallelized" -- which is how the real
caller surfaced: the declarative driver reaches the MoE through the layer's own
`Module.parallelize`, recursing into every child with the same `parallel_dims`, ep mesh
included. **EP was applied all along.** What was broken was the reporting, and EP applied
versus not applied looks identical from the loss, which is why a stale log line covered
it. `apply_ep_kimi_k3` now collects rather than parallelizes, and `verify_ep_applied`
runs after the driver and asserts each layer's routed-expert parameters are DTensors with
a non-replicate placement. Verified: `rc=0`, `EP: 12 MoE layer(s)`.

## Traps this session paid for

* **A log line is not a check.** A1 hid behind one for many green runs. Where a mechanism
  can be silently absent, assert it.
* **A green cell count is not coverage.** Two cells never ran; a second bug in my tally
  double-counted another pair, and the two cancelled to look correct.
* **Occupancy is not hiding**, and a cost ratio measured at one sequence length says
  nothing at another.
* **Do not work in a throwaway worktree.** `cd` into one to run training and the next
  edit lands there; I lost ~150 lines to `rm -rf` that way, and separately committed to
  the wrong repo from inside one.
* **Clear the dump folder per cell.** A killed run leaves a partial checkpoint the next
  one resumes from, failing with `Missing key in checkpoint state_dict:
  optimizer.state...step`, which reads as a defect in the cell.
* **Regex over identifiers needs word boundaries and cannot span multi-line calls.** Four
  separate mistakes: a class name rewritten, `nn.Linear(` matched as `Linear(`, a comment
  rewritten, and ten multi-line constructors half-converted. The routine that works:
  replace, `ast.parse`, read the diff's added and removed lines, grep the counter-example.
* **One GPU job at a time**, pytest included.
