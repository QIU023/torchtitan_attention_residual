3. `SEED=<a matrix seed dir> NP=2 bash kit/h200/run_grad_probe.sh`: std and MoonEP on one warm cache plus std again on a fresh cache as the floor row, then `grad_diff.py` per parameter group. The probe's `num_valid_tokens` handling is fixed; it had not run on this tree.
4. Only then the tables: `bash kit/h200/moonep_matrix_h200.sh dp2`, `dp2floor`, `dp4`, `dp4floor` (dp4floor was the one cell the last session did not finish), and `NP=4` self-tests and hot probe.
5. The open question the tables wait on: `dp2ep2_moonep` and `dp4ep4_moonep` differ from the standard dispatcher at step 1 (12.52362 against 12.52567; 12.56253 against 12.54318) while the fresh-cache floor rows are bitwise, so it is the transport's own arithmetic, not the cache.
