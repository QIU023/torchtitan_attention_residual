## What the numbers say

Peak memory falls as the sharding deepens, which is the expected direction and the one useful signal here: the four axis image cell (data parallel sharding by context parallel by tensor parallel with expert parallelism dividing their product) is the lightest at 4.11 GB, while pipeline parallelism alone is the heaviest at 10.99 GB because each stage holds a whole half of the model plus its token budget buffers. Context parallel alone is the slowest image cell at 48.4 s/step, and the three axis pipeline cell is the fastest at 14.9 s/step.

The image cells run far faster per step than the text cells of the same shapes (for instance context parallel at 48.4 s/step against 332.8 s/step) because the image campaign uses a much smaller batch: 8 prompts at n=2 with a 256 token prompt cap, against the text cells' larger stream. The two tables are therefore not comparable to each other, only within themselves.
