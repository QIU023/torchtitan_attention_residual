# What has to move to an H200 class box, and what must not

Supersedes the scope sections of `H200_HANDOFF_2026-07-21.md` and `H200_RUNBOOK.md`.
Their *recipes* stand; their premises about this hardware do not.

## The premise correction that motivates this file

Both of those documents were written on **box #1: 8x RTX 5090, 32 GB each, 256 GB
aggregate**. Work since 2026-07-24 has run on **box #2: 8x RTX 5060 Ti, 16 GB each,
128 GB aggregate, PCIe, Blackwell SM 12.0** (first recorded in
`PACKED_TP_VERIFICATION_2026-07-25.md`). Aggregate device memory HALVED between the two,
and neither H200 document knows it.

That invalidates one specific claim that has been repeated since:

> `PP_ADAPTER_AT_2P8T_2026-08-11.md` sec 5: "**Measure the GRPO peak at 48B**, not 2.8T.
> 4.4 is the most likely first failure and the cheapest to see."

48B in bf16 is 96 GB of weights alone; Adam states in fp32 add roughly 384 GB. On 128 GB
aggregate the full-parameter form of that experiment is not merely slow, it does not fit.
What box #2 can host is 48B **QLoRA** (MXFP4 base ~24 GB + adapters). Any statement of the
form "measurable today at 48B" needs to say which of the two it means.

## MUST move (this hardware cannot answer the question at all)

### 1. Every full-parameter 48B measurement
Weights 96 GB + optimizer ~384 GB against 128 GB aggregate. Includes the GRPO cache
high-water experiment in its full-parameter form, the fp32-master comparison, and any
step-time or MFU number anyone would quote for 48B training.

### 2. The AttnRes cache high-water curve
The term grows as `pp x vp x blocks x seq x hidden`. At the debug flavor hidden is 256, so
one cached block is 4 MiB and the whole in-flight set is noise. At 2.8T dims one block is
0.109 GiB at seq 8192 and 1.75 GiB at 131072. A curve measured at hidden=256 cannot be
extrapolated to either, so instrumenting it here is worth doing (the code belongs
wherever it is written) while the NUMBERS have to come from a box with real hidden size.

### 3. DEP exchange-buffer sizing against a real corpus
Defaults hold `8 x 32 x 32 = 8192` patches; `navit_resize` permits 512 per side, so ONE
full-resolution image is 262144 patches -- **32x the entire buffer**. Sizing this needs
real images at real resolution resident, and the fixed P2P buffer is paid on every hop
whether or not the batch uses it. 16 GB per rank is not where you tune that.

### 4. Anything about fabric or NVLink domains
Every bandwidth figure in `PP_DECISION_2026-08-11.md` is **arithmetic, not measurement** --
including the one that decides the file's answer (EP all-to-all at 923 GB/rank/step vs 166
GB of weight gather, and its pp-invariance). Box #2 is PCIe with no P2P; it cannot produce
a contradicting or confirming measurement. The whole "does PP widen DC coverage" question
is unmeasured on any hardware we have touched.

### 5. n_vit > 1 at depth
Verified exact from a shared checkpoint at 13 and 30 layers here, which is a correctness
result and worth having. What is untested is 93 layers with a 447M tower (401M encoder+pos_emb as report Table 1 counts it, plus a
46.1M projector) split across
stages -- that is a memory-and-depth question, not a correctness one, and the report
REQUIRES more than one ViT stage for DEP to mean what it says (sec 5.2.3, requirement 2).
The 30-layer rehearsal belongs here; the real depth does not.

## MUST NOT move (finishable here, and moving it wastes H200 hours)

| item | why it fits here |
|---|---|
| **The EP x TP merge regression** | Reproduces in 2 of 13 cells at debug scale, both arms. Diagnosis is source-reading plus two 8-GPU cells. |
| **`n_vit = 2` on the 30-layer flavor under FSDP+PP** | The report-fidelity gap that matters, and 30 layers fit. |
| **`#32` env-var topology -> config fields** | Pure plumbing across 8 adapter sites; PR-C's hard gate. Verification is the debug matrix. |
| **`#12` remaining 62 comment blocks** | Comments. CPU only. |
| **The 18-cell parallelism matrix itself** | Already 18/18 on both arms with DEP and dynamic CP; a byte-identical baseline exists. Re-running it on H200 proves nothing new. |
| **Numerics and exactness work** | KDA chunked-vs-recurrent equivalence, PP exactness from a shared checkpoint, vision preprocessing parity against the release -- all shape-independent or small. |
| **PR26 and the A/B/C/D extraction** | `llama3_debugmodel` and text editing. |

## The honest one-line split

Box #2 answers **"is it correct?"**. It cannot answer **"what does it cost?"** for anything
at K3 scale -- not memory, not step time, not bandwidth. Every remaining item is one or the
other, and sorting them that way is what this file is for.

## Two consequences for how results get stated

* **Nothing here has measured a cost at scale.** The project's claim stays what
  `CLAUDE.md` already requires: validated on 48B real weights and K3-faithful topology,
  scale-out is config-level. `PP_DECISION`'s tables are derivations and are labelled as
  such; do not let them migrate into a slide as measurements.
* **The "already verified, do NOT re-verify" list in `H200_HANDOFF_2026-07-21.md` was
  produced on 256 GB aggregate.** Items on it that are memory-shaped (MXFP4-base LoRA
  sharding at 15 packed bases, EP@896 112-experts-per-rank forward) held on box #1 and have
  not all been re-shown on box #2. Treat that list as box #1 evidence, not as
  hardware-independent.
