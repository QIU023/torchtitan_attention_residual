# TP/SP stacked on the CP PR (2026-09-09)

The maintainers' CP PR (4500, `acisseJZhong:kda_cp` = `2884d82a9`, stacked on fegin's 4322/4449/4450, base main `0ff246463` of 2026-09-03) covers the CP side of the spmd declarations PR (4492), so the TP/SP PR (4499) is re-based onto 4500's head as `tp_sp_on_4500` (fork). This note records what the stack carries, what the probes found, and what the PR body can claim.

## The branch

| commit | what | from |
|---|---|---|
| `clip_grad_norm_ groups parameters by mesh` | core: undeclared modules under TP hold grads on the fsdp-only mesh | 4499 as is |
| `tensor parallelism with sequence parallel on the declarations` | model.py / parallelize.py / sharding.py / the SP splice test; plus the TP-relevant parts of 4492's declarations commit (head splits: `local_head_split` in `kda.py`, MLA's local head count from the projection width, the tower-output DTensor wrap and SP gather/re-shard around the multimodal splice) | 4499 + 4492 hunks |
| `vision encoder under type checking at tp > 1` | core attention head split keeps the tower's declared TP type | 4499 as is |

Merge points against 4500: `sharding.py` keeps 4500's `set_kimi_k3_sharding_config` (CP local maps, tower over CP, MoE) and gains `enable_sp` and `declare_vision_encoder`; the TP declarations are the old `set_tensor_parallel_sharding_config`, with the inner-KDA local map re-declared head-sharded on tp and the per-head parameter gradients kept partial over cp as 4500 declares them. `update_from_config` calls 4500's function under `spmd_types` or `tp > 1` (the MoE declarations serve TP on partial_dtensor too; the tower only under spmd_types) and the TP function at `tp > 1`. `parallelize` keeps 4500's shape and adds `_sp_group` under spmd_types + SP. 4500's base predates the K3 EP merge (`9b5f60c40`, 2026-09-03), lists EP as unsupported, and its debug model is 24 layers; 4500 will need its own rebase and this stack follows it.

Local, uncommitted, on the run worktree only: the KDA SM90/SM100/SM103 guard lifted for SM120 (attn-gym's default `impl="fused"` is the portable Triton path, which requires capability 8.0; the guard is torchtitan's, and 4500 widened it to SM90 for H100), a lazy import of `torch.cuda._annotate_cuda_graph_trace` (main removed it in 4493; the nightly here lacks it), 9-layer flavor aliases, and the probe hacks (fp32 experts loop, gradient dump and exit, activation and KDA traces).

## The trap: "float32" that was bf16

`--training.dtype float32` on this tree gives float32 masters but bf16 compute: `parallelize` wraps dp1 in FSDP2 too, and `mixed_precision_param` defaults to bf16. The first three probe rounds compared bf16 forwards and found a 2.9e-3 gap in layer 0's `output_proj` that grew to 10 percent at the top of a 9-layer model and to gradient cosines of 0.8 to 0.95; it was two bf16 partial products summed against one bf16 matmul (both 1.7e-3 and 2.5e-3 from the fp64 product of the same dumped inputs). A probe is float32 only with `--training.mixed_precision_param float32 --training.mixed_precision_reduce float32` as well, and the trace prints the dtypes.

## True float32: the forward

9-layer alias, fp32 masters, fp32 compute, fp32 experts loop, 512 tokens in two micro-batches, one seed checkpoint, dp1 against tp2 (partial_dtensor, SP off) and tp2 (spmd_types, SP on):

| stage | dp1 vs tp2 (rel) |
|---|---|
| embedding output (after the multimodal splice) | bitwise |
| layer 0 KDA: input, gate, beta, conv weights, `A_log`, `dt_bias` | bitwise |
| layer 0 KDA: q / k / v projections (colwise) | 3.1e-7 |
| layer 0 KDA: kernel output, gated norm, `output_proj` | 6.5e-5 / 6.3e-5 / 6.2e-5 (`output_proj` 2.9e-7 from the fp64 product of its own input on both sides) |
| layer outputs 0 .. 8 | 3.3e-6, 1.4e-5, 1.7e-5, 1.7e-5, 2.1e-5, 2.3e-5, 2.5e-5, 2.6e-5, 2.7e-5 |
| final hidden state | 2.2e-5 (partial_dtensor), 2.3e-5 (spmd_types + SP) |

The 3e-7 on the projections is the colwise matmul's tiling (fp32); the kernel is not the source: attn-gym's `KDAKernel` on the same fp32 inputs with 16 heads in one call and with two calls of 8 heads is bitwise, and a 3e-7 relative perturbation of q/k/v moves its output by 1.8e-4 (1e-6 gives 2.1e-4, 1e-5 gives 6.9e-4), so the recurrence turns the projection's roundoff into the 6.5e-5 seen. Everything after that inherits it. Under bf16 compute the same chain starts at 2.9e-3 and ends at 10 percent, which is what the bf16 tables show.

## The tower under spmd_types at tp > 1

4500 declares the tower's projector colwise / rowwise (its CP cells run at tp=1, where a Partial on the tp axis is trivially replicated). At tp=2 with sequence parallel off, the fp32 gradient dump showed every vision-encoder parameter's gradient exactly doubled against dp1 (relative difference 1.0 on all 70) and the rest of the model at 1e-2, while the SP-on cell was fine: the rowwise exit leaves a Partial that nothing between the tower and the splice reduces. The branch declares the tower invariant on tp under spmd_types at tp > 1 (the 4499 declaration: it runs whole on every rank, as under partial_dtensor). TOWER_FIX_PLACEHOLDER

## What the PR body claims

The bar of the numerics rule: step-1 forward bitwise where the math is bitwise (everything up to the first sharded matmul), then every parameter inside the located floor. Here the located floor is the KDA recurrence's amplification of the sharded projection's fp32 roundoff; the bf16 five-step table sits next to the fp32 forward table and the gradient distribution.
