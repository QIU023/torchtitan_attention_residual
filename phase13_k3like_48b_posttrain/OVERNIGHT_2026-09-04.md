## 2026-09-05 early morning: the typechecked cells fail inside spmd_types; the entry conversion is guarded at tp > 1

- The declarations matrix on `spmd_decl_review1` `96eeb51c1` (chain `chain_decl.sh`: decl -> tpsp2 -> qb3b): dp1 partial_dtensor 12.52977 / 7.27107 / 2.98077, dp1 spmd_types bitwise the same, dp2 12.53137 / 7.31248 / 3.15823, dp2 x ep2 12.53146 / 7.20212 / 3.10296; the three typechecked cells (`kimi_k3_debugmodel_tc`: spmd_types with `--debug.spmd_typechecking`, AC off, the way 4446's B200 cell runs the flavor) all die in the first forward: `UnboundLocalError: cannot access local variable 'input_type'` at `spmd_types/_checker/__init__.py:2865` (`_typecheck_core`), reached from the model entry's `spmd.redistribute(block_residual_TND, tp_group, src=R, dst=I)`.
